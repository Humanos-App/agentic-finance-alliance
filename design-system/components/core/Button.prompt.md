Pill CTA used for every action on AFA surfaces. One primary per screen.

```jsx
<Button variant="fill" href="https://luma.com/g5d9e8zv">Get your seat at the next dinner.</Button>
<Button variant="cream" size="lg">Become a partner for the next dinner.</Button>
```

`fill` on cream, `cream` on deep green or photos, `line` as a secondary on dark, `ghost` as a secondary on cream. 15px / 500, 12px 24px (lg: 16px, 14px 28px). Hover opacity .86. Button text is a full sentence with a period.
